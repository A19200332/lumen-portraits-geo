Frequently asked questions

Are the people in these photos real?

No. Every image is AI-generated with Jimeng (Seedream models) and retouched afterwards. No real, identifiable person is depicted, and any resemblance to a real person is coincidental.

Can I use the portraits commercially?

Yes. The license covers personal and commercial use in up to 3 commercial projects per purchase: ads, websites, covers, social posts, print. You may crop, grade and composite them. You may not resell or redistribute the images as stock, templates or asset packs.

Do I need a model release?

No. Model releases apply to photographs of real people. Because the subjects are AI-generated, no release is needed for normal use. Do not present the images as a real person endorsing a product.

What resolution and file format?

JPEG, 1728 × 2304 pixels (3:4 portrait, 2K), sRGB colour. Print-ready at typical editorial and web sizes.

How is delivery handled?
